Acme Corp Employee Handbook

Working Hours
Our standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Employees can work from home two days a week after completing their probation period.

Leave Policy
Every full-time employee gets 20 days of paid leave per year. Up to 5 unused leave days can be carried over to the next year. Sick leave is separate and is 10 days per year.

Interview Process
Our hiring process has three rounds: a take-home assignment, a technical interview and a final HR round. Technical interviews are 60 minutes long and are conducted over Google Meet. Candidates can book an interview slot through our assistant.

Benefits
All employees get health insurance for themselves and their family, a yearly learning budget of 500 USD and a new laptop on joining.
